About Me

I'm an AI researcher and engineer dedicated to building trustworthy, interpretable systems for medicine and healthcare. I currently work as a Project Research Scientist at the Amity Centre for Artificial Intelligence, Amity University, Noida, where I work on AI research projects in collaboration with Dr. Lal PathLabs. I completed my M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence from Amity University, Noida, after a B.Tech in Computer Science from VIT Vellore.

My research sits at the intersection of computational pathology, medical imaging, large language models, and clinical decision support, spanning histopathology whole-slide images, electron microscopy, endoscopy, biomedical question answering, and quantum machine learning. My work on retrieval-augmented biomedical QA has been accepted in BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making (Impact Factor 5.5), alongside papers at ICAICCIT 2025 and IICTDS 2025, and I hold an Indian design patent for an AI-driven radiology system. Previously, I was a part-time Artificial Intelligence Engineer at Winniio AB (Sweden), building clinical AI products.

Alongside research, I have conducted hands-on workshops on deep learning, LLMs, agentic AI, and AI automation for undergraduate and graduate students. I was also recognised among the top performers (4th place) at the AI Campus Hackathon organised by Redrob at Amity University, Noida.

On the technical side, I work across the modern AI stack, from PyTorch, Hugging Face, and timm to pathology foundation models (UNI, CONCH, Virchow), multiple instance learning, PennyLane, FAISS, LangChain, and Docker, and I am just as comfortable wrangling messy clinical datasets as I am fine-tuning models or designing end-to-end systems.

CogniMed · Evidence-Grounded Biomedical QA

A retrieval-augmented pipeline for tri-class (yes/no/maybe) biomedical QA. Combines PubMedBERT dense retrieval over a 20,000-passage corpus with BioGPT-Large fine-tuned via LoRA, focal loss, post-hoc calibration, and a five-layer clinical safety framework. Reached 73.5% accuracy (MCC 0.509) on a held-out split; accepted in BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making.

QMT-PAT · Quantum Multi-Task Gastritis Classification

A hybrid quantum-classical model combining a fine-tuned ViT-B/16 encoder, patient-level attention aggregation, and a 16-qubit task-structured variational circuit to jointly predict H. pylori status, atrophy presence, atrophy distribution, and gastritis type from endoscopic images. Achieved the best patient-level balanced accuracy (0.791, AUROC 0.920) on the EGID dataset (229 patients, 5,883 images).
